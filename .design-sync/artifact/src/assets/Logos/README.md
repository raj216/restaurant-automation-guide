# Logos

- `kadmivo-mark.svg` — the transfer mark for paper and white grounds: two brackets in ink #20282b, the register line and transfer bar in terracotta #a74722. 2.1 stroke on a 48 grid, square caps; shown at 30px in the header.
- `kadmivo-mark-inverse.svg` — the same mark for dark grounds: the brackets in white #fffdf8, the terracotta strokes unchanged.

The wordmark is live text, not a file: "Kadmivo" in Manrope 800 at 16px, tracked -0.04em, 9px after the mark. In code, use the `BrandMark` component (it draws both, and animates the strokes when motion is on).
